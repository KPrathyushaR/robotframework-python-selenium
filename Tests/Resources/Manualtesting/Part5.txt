Tips for Scenario-Based Questions

When answering in an interview, structure your response like this:

Understand the requirement – Clarify the feature and business rules.
Identify positive scenarios – Expected successful flows.
Identify negative scenarios – Invalid inputs and error conditions.
Consider boundary conditions – Minimum, maximum, and edge values.
Think about security – Authentication, authorization, input validation.
Check usability and compatibility – Different browsers, devices, and accessibility.
Include performance if relevant – Response time and concurrency.

This structured approach demonstrates that you think like an experienced QA engineer rather than simply listing test cases.
