2. Explain your testing process from requirement to release.
Answer

My testing process follows these steps:

Step 1: Requirement Analysis
Read BRD, FRD, or User Stories.
Clarify doubts with the Business Analyst or Product Owner.
Identify test scenarios.
Step 2: Test Planning
Estimate effort.
Identify risks.
Decide testing scope.
Step 3: Test Case Design
Write test cases.
Review with the team.
Prepare test data.
Step 4: Environment Setup
Verify QA environment.
Validate database.
Ensure APIs are available.
Step 5: Test Execution
Execute test cases.
Record results.
Capture screenshots.
Log defects.
Step 6: Defect Tracking
Report defects in Jira.
Participate in defect triage.
Retest fixes.
Step 7: Regression Testing
Execute regression suite.
Validate impacted modules.
Step 8: Test Closure
Prepare Test Summary Report.
Share testing metrics.
Sign off for release.

7. What is Risk-Based Testing?
Answer

Risk-Based Testing focuses testing efforts on the areas with the highest business impact or technical risk.

Example

For an e-commerce application:

Highest Risk:

Payment
Checkout
Login

Lower Risk:

About Us
Contact Us

If time is limited, test high-risk modules first.

8. How do you coordinate with developers when a defect is rejected?
Sample Answer

First, I verify that the issue is reproducible. Then I collect evidence such as screenshots, logs, videos, and database records. I discuss the issue with the developer to understand their perspective. If there is still disagreement, I review the requirement with the Business Analyst or Product Owner and participate in a defect triage meeting to reach a final decision.

12. Describe a critical defect you found.
Sample Answer

During regression testing, I found that after a successful payment, duplicate orders were being created. This could have resulted in customers being charged twice. I immediately logged the defect with screenshots, logs, and reproduction steps. The development team identified a duplicate API call, fixed it, and we verified the solution through retesting and regression testing before release.

13. How do you perform regression testing?
Answer

I first identify the impacted modules, then execute the regression suite covering both affected and dependent functionality. I prioritize critical business flows such as login, payments, and user management. Where automation is available, I run the automated regression suite and manually verify high-risk scenarios that require manual validation.
